Sprig

An open-source desktop client for WhatsApp Web and Google Messages on Linux.

Version 1.0.0
Open Source & Privacy First: Sprig is a fully open-source desktop client that brings WhatsApp Web and Google Messages to Ubuntu and other Linux distributions. It connects directly to official web endpoints (web.whatsapp.com and messages.google.com) in isolated sandbox partitions and runs no intermediary servers: all communications travel directly between your device and the respective messaging services. Sprig collects no telemetry or analytics.

Support Sprig Development

Sprig is 100% free and open-source. Donations support continuous development, new features, and Linux packaging.
